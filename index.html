About Me 👋🏻

👋🏻 I am a Master's student at the School of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (AI&DS), University of Science and Technology of China (USTC) , working at the SCAI LAB and advised by Prof. Jingrun Chen and Prof. Congcong Zhu.

🔬 My research focuses on Large Language Models (LLMs), Embodied AI and Agentic AI, with a particular focus on agent-driven physical simulation.

🚀 I am passionate about building LLM-powered autonomous agents that plan, generate, and iteratively self-correct physics simulations, producing verifiable, ground-truth trajectory data to train and evaluate embodied AI systems.

Open Source Projects 🛠️

PhysPi — AI-Native Physics Simulation Agent

PhysPi turns natural-language scene descriptions into executable physics simulations, rendered videos, and verifiable trajectory data. It combines an LLM-powered agent layer with the Genesis physics engine to plan scenes, generate simulation code, validate runs, inspect artifacts, and iteratively self-correct toward reproducible results.
